LEARN PHOTOGRAPHY

ISO

ISO controls how sensitive the camera is to light. A higher ISO makes photos brighter in the dark, but can add grain.

APERTURE

Aperture controls the opening of the lens. A wide aperture (small f-number) lets in more light and blurs the background.

SHUTTER SPEED

Shutter speed controls how long the camera captures light. A fast shutter freezes motion; a slow shutter can blur it.

EXPOSURE

Exposure is how bright or dark a photo looks. It is affected together by ISO, aperture and shutter speed.
